DMFC Hybrid APU & Energy Bank

50W / 130W / 200W / 500W net DC power at 12V or 24V DC. Hybrid APU with integrated energy bank: fuel cell delivers steady load while lithium buffer handles peak transient demands. Methanol fuel provides approx. 1 kWh per litre without requiring external hydrogen reformers or high-pressure gas cylinders.

Applications: Vehicle APU, silent watch, turret electronics, remote sensors, tactical UAV-UGV charging.
Power banks: 1,500 - 10,000 Wh; optional 110 / 230 V AC inverter; vehicle engine-start support.
Environmental: Ruggedized aluminum housing qualified to MIL-STD-810G test configuration.
