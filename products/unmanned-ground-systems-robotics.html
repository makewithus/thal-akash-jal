EXTREME MOBILITY

Chassis-less Multi-Terrain UGV (4WD / 6WD)

Monocoque all-terrain unmanned ground vehicle with independent hub motors.

Chassis-less structure eliminates structural weak points and maximizes payload
Independent electric hub drive on all wheels (4WD or 6WD modular options)
Traverses high altitude snow, mud, rocky inclines, and desert dunes
Modular top deck accommodates weapon mounts, CASEVAC stretchers, or ISR masts

5t - 25t HEAVY HAULER

Heavy-Duty Autonomous Transporter

Autonomous transporter for forward ammunition, fuel, and heavy logistics transfer.

Carries payloads from 5 tonnes up to 25 tonnes across unpaved terrain
LiDAR, millimeter-wave radar, and camera fusion for GPS-denied navigation
Multi-axle synchronized steering with Crab-walk and Zero-turn capability
Reduces convoy troop exposure in contested frontline logistic corridors

RAIL INFRASTRUCTURE

Railway Track Inspection Robot

Autonomous crawler with ultrasonic, laser profile, and visual defect sensors.

Autonomous crawling along rail tracks detecting crack propagation & wear
Laser profile sensor checks rail gauge width, cant angle, and head profile
AI vision module detects missing clips, displaced fasteners, and ballast defects
Single-operator deployable; battery autonomy for full maintenance shifts
View Track Robot
IP66 RECONNAISSANCE

Throwable Spherical Observation Robot

Tough spherical throwable surveillance robot for SWAT, CT, and building clearance.

Survives drops from 5 meters onto solid concrete without damage (IP66)
Twin internal drive wheels enable self-righting and silent indoor traversal
360-degree daylight and thermal night-vision cameras with two-way audio
Transmits encrypted low-latency live video up to 200m through thick walls
View Throwable Bot
NAVAL DOCKYARD UGV

Mobile Naval Cable-Handling UGV

Heavy-duty tracked robotic cable handler for naval drydocks and shore-power berths.

Handles thick high-voltage shore power and fueling cables automatically
Prevents severe human back and limb crush injuries during vessel mooring
Heavy-duty rubber tracks provide extreme traction on wet and greasy slipways
Integrated spooling drum with automatic electronic tension feedback control
