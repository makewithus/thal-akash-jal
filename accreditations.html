Tested, Certified and Compliant with National and International Defence Standards:

DGQA Tested & Certified (Kanpur)

Tested and certified by the Directorate General of Quality Assurance (DGQA), Ministry of Defence, Kanpur. Ballistic and fire testing conducted with live ammunition (7.62x39mm AK-47, 7.62x51mm NATO, 5.56x45mm INSAS) with zero explosion.

UL & UN Certified (SwRI, USA)

Tested and certified by Southwest Research Institute (SwRI, San Antonio, Texas) and Underwriters Laboratories (UL). UN Packaging Specification: UN 3A1/Y/170/**/GB/6906 for explosive-suppression fuel canisters and tactical containment systems.

Bureau Veritas Design Appraisal

Technical design appraisal and safety validation by Bureau Veritas for commercial explosion-resistant mobile fuel bowsers, light commercial vehicle skids, and solar fuel stations.

ISO 9001, ISO 14001 & ISO 45001

Integrated Management Systems certified across Quality Management (ISO 9001:2015), Environmental Sustainability (ISO 14001:2015), and Occupational Health & Safety (ISO 45001:2018) for defence consulting and engineering services.

NIJ 0101.06 & 0106.01 Ballistic Standards

Full compliance across ballistic helmets (PASGT, ACH, High Cut) and body armour systems from NIJ Level IIIA to Level IV. Validated by NABL-accredited test laboratories with a 5-year ballistic warranty.

STANAG 4569 (NATO AEP-55)

NATO-standard ballistic and blast protection compliance up to Level 2 and Level 3 for tactical wheeled/tracked vehicles, modular field shelters, containerized cabins, and naval vessel armouring.

MIL-STD-810H & MIL-STD-461G

Environmental qualification covering -40°C to +55°C operating temperatures, IP67/IP68 ingress sealing, high shock, vibration, and EMI/EMC shielding for electronic warfare, antennas, SDR, and unmanned platforms.

DAP 2020 Make in India (IDDM)

Fully compliant with Indigenous Design, Development and Manufacturing (IDDM) criteria under Defence Acquisition Procedure 2020. Over 700+ installations successfully deployed across Indian Armed Forces and government infrastructure.
