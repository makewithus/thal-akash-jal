WHO WE ARE

Leadership and Professional Expertise

Defence Expertise. Government Insight. Business Excellence.

TAJ is led by professionals with a unique blend of military, governmental and corporate experience. The founding leadership combines over 2 decades of distinguished service in the Armed Forces with over 6 years of corporate leadership, providing deep expertise in strategic planning, logistics, procurement, stakeholder engagement, operational management and business transformation.

The organization is supported by a network of veteran Armed Forces personnel and former Government officers specialised in logistics, procurement, supply chain management, warehousing, contract administration, project execution, technology adoption and institutional governance. This combination of operational discipline, public-sector understanding and commercial expertise enables practical, result-oriented and sustainable solutions across diverse sectors.
