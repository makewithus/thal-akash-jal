UK Announces New Defence Drone Strategy

The UK Ministry of Defence (MoD) has launched the United Kingdom's approach to Defence Uncrewed Systems. 'At the heart of this the serious commitment to allocate £4.5 billion to fund R&D and delivery of next generation UAS and C-UAS.
